<section class="section">
  <h2>What this behavioral section includes</h2>
  <p>The section helps you prepare your own story: how to introduce yourself, explain why you want the role, describe your biggest technical achievement, and talk honestly about strengths and weaknesses using the STAR method.</p>
  <p>It also covers working with people and process: handling disagreement with a teammate or lead, giving and receiving code review feedback, Scrum roles, events, and artifacts, Scrum versus Kanban, and writing user stories with acceptance criteria and a Definition of Done.</p>
</section>

<section class="section">
  <h2>Representative behavioral and agile interview questions</h2>
  <ol class="topic-list">
    <li>Tell me about yourself.</li>
    <li>Why do you want this role?</li>
    <li>What is the STAR method?</li>
    <li>What is your biggest technical achievement?</li>
    <li>What are your strengths and weaknesses?</li>
    <li>How do you handle disagreement with a teammate or lead?</li>
    <li>How do you give and receive code review feedback?</li>
    <li>What are the Scrum roles, events, and artifacts?</li>
    <li>When would you choose Kanban over Scrum?</li>
    <li>What is the difference between acceptance criteria and a Definition of Done?</li>
  </ol>
</section>
